# Evidence & Reconstruction Notes

This file documents the evidence boundary for the portfolio case.

## Reported project information

The case summary available to the author identifies:

- The GOMP / smart_edu university platform context
- Digital certificate generation and automated PDF generation
- Automated data validation and certificate lifecycle handling
- Reported baseline: 20% automated generation, 80% manual processing, and one business day for issuance
- Reported outcome: 85% automated generation, 15% manual processing, and 10 minutes for issuance

These values are reported project figures from the available candidate CV/project summary; this repository does not independently audit them.

## Reconstructed scenario

The scenario that automation coverage differed by certificate type and that requests without an applicable template used a manual route is a working hypothesis selected to structure this case study. It is not confirmed by the available source material.

## Proposed portfolio artifacts

The following are designed for this portfolio case and must not be described as original internal Be Smart documents:

- AS-IS and TO-BE diagrams
- Root-cause hypotheses
- Business rules and status model
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- OpenAPI contract

## How to use this case in interviews

Present the reported context and outcomes accurately. When discussing the process model or technical artifacts, explain that they are reconstructed/proposed portfolio artifacts based on the scenario, not a claim to possess or reproduce the original company's proprietary specifications.

Do not add unsupported details such as actual certificate names, internal API paths, database schemas, integrations, or historical root causes unless they can be independently verified.
